A lump under the skin is a common concern. Most are harmless, such as lipoma or cysts, but some may require medical evaluation—especially if they grow, become painful, or change in appearance.

如果发现肿块变大或有变化，建议尽早检查。

What Could a Lump Be?

  • Lipoma: Soft, movable, painless
  • Cyst: May become inflamed or infected
  • Abscess: Painful swelling due to infection
  • Benign growth: Non-cancerous tissue
  • Suspicious lump: Hard, irregular, fast-growing

When Should You Worry?

  • Growing quickly
  • Hard or fixed
  • Painful
  • Skin changes or ulceration
  • Bleeding or discharge
  • Persistent over weeks

These signs do not always mean cancer—but they should not be ignored.

Treatment Options

  • Observation for harmless lumps
  • Medication for infection
  • Minor surgery under local anaesthesia

Most procedures are quick, safe, and performed as day surgery with minimal downtime.

Common Questions

Is a lump always cancer?

No. Most lumps are benign.

Do I need surgery?

Only if the lump is growing, symptomatic, or uncertain.

Will removal leave a scar?

A small scar is expected but minimised with proper technique.
