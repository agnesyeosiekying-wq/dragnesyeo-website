Explore Your Keloid Treatment Options

If a keloid is bothering you, we are here to listen. We offer a range of treatment options, with recommendations guided by an assessment of your scar and a discussion of your concerns.

  • Steroid injection sessions
  • 5-fluorouracil (5-FU) injections
  • Laser treatment
  • Surgical removal in selected cases

Conventional scar-care options will also be discussed during consultation, including which may be appropriate for you and how they may fit into your treatment plan.

Assessment is required before treatment. We will discuss suitable options, potential benefits, risks and whether a course of sessions or a combination of treatments may be appropriate. Results vary between individuals.

Request a Keloid Consultation

Every scar is different. Your assessment helps guide the treatment discussion.

Scars form as part of normal healing, but some may become raised, thickened, or extend beyond the original wound. Keloids and hypertrophic scars can be uncomfortable and may continue to grow over time.

Types of Scars

  • Keloid: Extends beyond original wound（瘢痕疙瘩）
  • Hypertrophic scar: Raised but stays within wound boundary（疤痕增生）
  • Atrophic scar: Sunken (e.g. acne scars)
  • Normal scar: Flat and fades over time

When Should You Seek Treatment?

如果疤痕持续增生、发痒或影响外观，建议尽早处理。

  • Scar continues to grow
  • Itchy or painful
  • Raised or thickened
  • Affects appearance or confidence

Planning Your Scar Treatment

Your consultation is an opportunity to discuss your scar, any previous treatments and what matters most to you. The recommended approach will depend on the scar type, location and your individual circumstances.

We will explain the proposed treatment and follow-up plan so you can make an informed decision.

Common Questions 常见问题

Will my scar go away completely?疤痕会完全消失吗？
Complete scar removal cannot be promised. Your consultation will cover realistic expectations and the limitations of treatment.无法保证疤痕完全消失。咨询时，医生会说明合理的治疗预期及治疗的局限。
Are keloids dangerous?瘢痕疙瘩有危险吗？
They are not cancerous but can continue growing and cause discomfort.瘢痕疙瘩不是癌症，但可能持续增生并引起不适。
Is treatment painful?治疗会痛吗？
The level of discomfort depends on the treatment and the individual. We will discuss what to expect and options for managing discomfort before treatment.不适程度因治疗方式及个人情况而异。治疗前，医生会说明可能出现的感受及减轻不适的方法。
