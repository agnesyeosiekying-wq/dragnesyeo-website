Is Your Mole Dangerous?

Most moles are harmless—but some may be early signs of skin cancer. Knowing what to look out for can help you seek treatment early.

如果你的痣出现变化（大小、颜色、形状），建议尽早评估。

Early detection makes a significant difference — small changes should not be ignored.

Moles are common and usually harmless. However, certain changes in size, colour, or shape may indicate a need for medical evaluation. Early detection of skin cancer significantly improves treatment outcomes.

Warning Signs of a Dangerous Mole

危险痣的常见表现包括大小、颜色或形状的变化。

  • Asymmetry (uneven shape)
  • Irregular or blurred borders
  • Multiple colours or uneven pigmentation
  • Increasing size
  • Bleeding, crusting, or ulceration
  • New mole appearing in adulthood

What Doctors Look For (ABCDE Rule)

  • A – Asymmetry
  • B – Border irregularity
  • C – Colour variation
  • D – Diameter > 6mm
  • E – Evolving (changing over time)

When Should a Mole Be Removed?

  • Suspicious changes
  • Bleeding or discomfort
  • Rapid growth
  • Cosmetic concern

Removal is usually a minor procedure done under local anaesthesia, with minimal downtime.
