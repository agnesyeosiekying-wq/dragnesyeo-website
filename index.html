Understanding Treatment Costs

Surgical cost depends on the procedure, complexity, facility, anaesthesia, implants or specialised equipment, and the care required after surgery. Following assessment, you will receive a plan and cost estimate relevant to your needs before deciding whether to proceed.

Common Questions

Do I need to know which procedure I want before consultation?

No. It is enough to explain what concerns you. Assessment determines whether surgery, a non-surgical option or no treatment is most appropriate.

Will the result look natural?

The plan is tailored to your anatomy and preferred degree of change. Results vary between individuals, and realistic possibilities and limitations will be discussed during consultation.

Will surgery leave a scar?

Every surgical incision produces a scar. Incisions are planned carefully, and scar care is incorporated into follow-up, but final scar quality also depends on healing and individual biology.

Can I enquire privately before booking?

Yes. You may begin with a confidential WhatsApp enquiry. A clinical diagnosis or final surgical plan, however, requires an appropriate medical consultation and examination.
