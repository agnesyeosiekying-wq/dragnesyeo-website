SCAR MANAGEMENT

Keloid & Scar Treatment

Scars can be raised, thickened, or persistent over time. Understanding the type of scar you have helps guide the most effective treatment.

Ask About Your Scar on WhatsApp

Not all scars behave the same — personalised treatment gives better outcomes.

Scars form as part of normal healing, but some may become raised, thickened, or extend beyond the original wound. Keloids and hypertrophic scars can be uncomfortable and may continue to grow over time.

Types of Scars

  • Keloid: Extends beyond original wound, continues growing
  • Hypertrophic scar: Raised but stays within wound boundary
  • Atrophic scar: Sunken (e.g. acne scars)
  • Normal scar: Flat and fades over time

When Should You Seek Treatment?

  • Scar continues to grow
  • Itchy or painful
  • Raised or thickened
  • Affects appearance or confidence

Treatment Options

  • Silicone gel or sheets
  • Steroid injections
  • Laser therapy
  • Surgical revision (selected cases)

Treatment is tailored based on the scar type, location, and individual response.

Common Questions

Will my scar go away completely?
Scars can improve significantly but may not disappear completely.
Are keloids dangerous?
They are not cancerous but can continue growing and cause discomfort.
Is treatment painful?
Most treatments are well tolerated with minimal discomfort.

Concerned About Your Scar?

Early treatment can help control growth and improve appearance.
