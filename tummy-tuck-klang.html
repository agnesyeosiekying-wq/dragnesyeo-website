What is Abdominoplasty?

Abdominoplasty, commonly known as a tummy tuck, removes excess skin and fat from the abdomen while tightening weakened muscles.

This creates a smoother, firmer abdominal profile, especially for patients whose contour has not improved with diet or exercise alone.

Key Benefits

Flatter Contour

Achieve a toned, sculpted midsection that enhances your natural proportions.

Muscle Repair

Tighten separated abdominal muscles often caused by pregnancy or weight changes.

Renewed Confidence

Feel comfortable and confident in clothing and everyday life again.

Who is it for?

  • Loose abdominal skin
  • Post-pregnancy changes
  • Separated abdominal muscles
  • Stable body weight

Procedure Options

Mini Tummy Tuck

Targets the lower abdomen with a smaller incision.

  • Shorter recovery
  • Smaller incision
  • Less invasive

Full Tummy Tuck

Addresses the entire abdomen with muscle tightening.

  • Full correction
  • Muscle repair
  • Long-lasting results

Extended Tummy Tuck

Includes flanks for more comprehensive contouring.

  • 360° contouring
  • Flank tightening
  • Dramatic results

Common Questions

Am I a good candidate?
Ideal candidates have excess skin and stable body weight.
How long is recovery?
Most return to light activities within 2 weeks.
Will there be visible scarring?
Scars are placed low and fade over time.
Can it be combined?
Yes, often combined with liposuction or breast procedures.
